Drivers

The efficiency score compares each driver's Wh/km with what the household typically uses on trips at similar speeds, so town and highway driving are compared fairly. 100 is average and higher is better. A driver needs 50 km of trips to be rated. Each trip starts with the first driver; change it in the Trips table.
